Open source · Windows · macOS · Linux

Your address list, checked every minute.

Load hundreds of Bitcoin addresses. The app checks them on a loop and sends one Telegram or email alert the first time a balance appears. Keys never leave your machine because there are no keys: addresses only.

Download the appCheck addresses in your browser

Specification

01 Input
Add one by one, paste in bulk, or drop a .txt / .csv. P2PKH, P2SH, bech32 and Taproot, each verified by checksum. Duplicates and typos are reported, not swallowed.
02 Lookup
Batched requests to blockchain.info with an automatic mempool.space fallback. Rate limits respected.
03 Watch
Re-scan on your interval (default 60 s). Balances shown in BTC and sats with a running total.
04 Alerts
Telegram and SMTP email, once per address, on first sight of a balance. Credentials stay in your OS config folder, owner-only.
05 CLI
Run btc-balance-checker --cli address.txt for the classic terminal loop.
06 Local
List, history and settings live on your disk. No account, no telemetry.
